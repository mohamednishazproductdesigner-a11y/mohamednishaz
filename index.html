HOW I WORK

My Design Principles

01

Balance users and business

Great design meets real user needs while supporting business goals. Both deserve a place in every decision.

02

Make room for different perspectives

Design invites different opinions. Listening carefully leads to stronger, more thoughtful solutions.

03

Let feedback shape the work

Feedback is part of the process. I use it to question assumptions and improve the experience.

04

Keep learning, keep improving

The first version is a starting point. Testing and iteration help the right solution take shape.
